Past papers

Official AQA question papers and mark schemes are free on AQA's website. Each link below opens the subject's page; look for assessment resources, then question papers and mark schemes. AQA keeps most papers online for about three years, and the very latest series may not be public yet.

  1. Sit the paper in one go, timed, with nothing open but what's allowed in the exam.
  2. Mark it honestly with the official mark scheme.
  3. Log your mark below and note the topics where you lost marks, then revise those in the notes.

Links go to AQA's own website, which is the only place to download the papers. This site doesn't host or copy AQA material. Your logged marks are saved in this browser only.
